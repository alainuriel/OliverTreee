Music, Personality and a Unique Style

About Oliver Tree

Hello everyone. Today I’m going to talk about Oliver Tree.

Oliver Tree was a singer, producer and director from California.

Oliver Tree

He became known for his unique style, funny personality, and unusual music videos.

He made music mixing pop, rock, hip-hop and electronic. His biggest hits are Life Goes On, Miss You and Alien Boy. He also directed his own funny and creative music videos.

Some of his most popular songs are “Hurt,” “Life Goes On,” and “Miss You.”

Why I Like It

I admire him because he was 100% authentic, he didn't try to be perfect and he didn't seek anyone's approval. He proved that ugly is beautiful.
